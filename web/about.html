Background

The task

This project reframes the Medical Segmentation Decathlon's Task06_Lung tumour CT as a patch classifier — tumour-present versus non-tumour — and scores attribution against the real tumour segmentation mask, not a hand-drawn region of interest.

Why it matters: a model can be right for the wrong reason. A classifier can reach high accuracy while keying off scanner artifacts, patient positioning, or image borders instead of the lesion itself. Attribution, checked against honest quantitative metrics rather than eyeballed heat-maps, is how you find out whether the network actually localises the tumour.
